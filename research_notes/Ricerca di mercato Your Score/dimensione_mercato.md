# Market size and structure: amateur racket-sport tournaments in Europe and Italy (for a courtside digital result-entry and certified scoresheet product)

Research date: 22 October 2026. Method note: direct page fetching was blocked in this session (egress proxy refused fitp.it, agi.it, ilfoglio.it, europeanpickleballfederation.org), so figures below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages. Each figure carries its reference year. Treat every number as "reported by source X" and check the primary PDF before external use, especially the FITP Report integrato 2024 (https://www.fitp.it/media/File_pdf/20260603_Report_integrato_2024.pdf), which I could not open.

## 1. FITP umbrella in Italy: members, clubs, courts, tournaments (the core addressable base)

### Takeaway
FITP (tennis, padel, pickleball, beach tennis) is the single gatekeeper for Italian federal amateur and mid-level racket tournaments. In 2024 it reported about 1.15M members, about 4,000 clubs, 6,352 tennis tournaments and 1,367 padel tournaments, plus about 13,100 tennis and 2,270 padel teams in team championships. That is roughly 7,700 sanctioned individual tournaments a year before TPRA and pickleball.

### Cited findings (official FITP figures, as relayed by press)
- FITP members: 1,151,769 in December 2024, the first year above 1 million, +266% vs 2020 (FITP "Report Tennis e Padel 2024"). Sources: [Sport e Finanza, Jul 2025](https://www.sportefinanza.it/2025/07/04/italia-report-fitp-numeri-tennis-padel-tesserati-appasionati-impatto-economico/), [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/sport-economy/atp-finals-torino-2025-numeri/938742/), [FITP news on the report](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-internazionale/report-tennis-padel-2024-fitp)
- FITP members 2025: about 1,254,000 (vs FIGC 1,497,000). About 6.2M Italians played tennis or padel in 2025 (practitioners, not members). Source: [Il Foglio, 16 Apr 2026](https://www.ilfoglio.it/roma-capoccia/2026/04/16/news/racchette-romane-boom-di-appassionati-e-giocatori-amatoriali-roma-ama-il-tennis-non-solo-gli-internazionali--276635)
- Conflicting earlier figure: 799,425 members and a record 4,012 affiliated clubs at 26 Sep 2024. This is a mid-season snapshot, so it differs from the December total. Source: [SportFace](https://www.sportface.it/tennis/fitp-nuovo-record-societa-affiliate-quasi-ottocentomila-tesserati/2697440)
- Clubs and courts (2024 report): 4,096 affiliated clubs, 9,323 tennis courts, 4,982 padel facilities or courts (the source wording is ambiguous). Source: [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/sport-economy/atp-finals-torino-2025-numeri/938742/)
- **Tournaments 2024 (official): 6,352 tennis tournaments with 439,961 "giocatori coinvolti" (likely entries), 1,367 padel tournaments with 62,541 participants. Team championships: 26 tennis championships with 13,103 teams, 15 padel championships with 2,270 teams.** Source: [Sport e Finanza, citing FITP Report Tennis e Padel 2024](https://www.sportefinanza.it/2025/07/04/italia-report-fitp-numeri-tennis-padel-tesserati-appasionati-impatto-economico/)
- Historical: 6,475 tournaments and championships organised in Italy in 2022, +37% vs 2021. The mix was 52% Open, 28% Under, 3% Over and 17% other. Source: [FITP Report integrato 2022 (PDF)](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Federazione/File_Pdf/20240116_Report_integrato_2022_w.pdf), as relayed by search extract
- FITP revenue 2025: over €230M, surpassing FIGC. Il Foglio cites €243,459,085. Sources: [Il Sole 24 Ore EN](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/federtennis-2025-grossed-230-million-surpassed-football-AI7mRzW), [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/sport-economy/fitp-sorpasso-storico-ricavi-figc/952511/)
- Regional example: FITP Sicily ended 2025 with 350 clubs and over 35,000 members. Source: [La Sicilia](https://www.lasicilia.it/news/sport/3010741/successo-a-enna-della-festa-delle-racchette-siciliane-sul-palco-del-teatro-neglia-premiati-tutti-i-protagonisti-della-stagione-2025.html)

### Inferences
- The figure of about 440k "players involved" across 6,352 tennis tournaments implies about 69 entries per tournament on average. That fits typical Italian open/limitato draws (singles plus doubles, several categories).
- The 2024 counts most likely cover agonistic (sanctioned) tournaments only. TPRA amateur events seem to be counted separately (see section 3), but this is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official 2024 integrated report PDF to confirm the breakdown (open, limitati, TPRA, pickleball), the number of giudici arbitri, or whether TPRA is included.
- No 2025 tournament count was found.

## 2. Padel: Italy and Europe

### Takeaway
Italy is Europe's second padel market after Spain. It has roughly 10,000 courts and about 3,700 clubs (mid-2025, FIP/Playtomic data), about 5,300 FITP-affiliated courts and about 240,000 FITP padel members (2025). Federal padel tournaments numbered 1,367 in 2024. Europe holds about 70% of the world's 77k courts.

### Cited findings
- Italy, 2025: 5,321 FITP-affiliated padel courts and nearly 240,000 FITP padel members, with Lazio leading at about 2,014 courts. Source: FIP research department, cited by [AGI, 12 Jul 2026](https://www.agi.it/sport/news/2026-07-12/campi-da-padel-italia-37986662/)
- Italy, mid-2025: 10,220 courts across 3,716 clubs, +29% since 2022. Source: secondary, citing FIP World Padel Report / Playtomic: [sgbonline](https://sgbonline.com/exec-study-finds-padel-boasting-robust-growth/), [The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/playtomic-global-padel-report-2025/)
- About 9,700 padel courts in Italy in 2024, close to the tennis court count. Source: [Il Fatto Quotidiano, Mar 2025](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2025/03/07/effetto-sinner-nuovi-tennisti-non-si-trovano-campi-crescita-padel-piu-conveniente/7901057/)
- Italy players: estimated at about 1.5M (estimate, not official). Source: [Racket Business](https://racketbusiness.com/p/padel-in-europe-a-three-lane-motorway-of-growth)
- Playtomic/PwC Global Padel Report 2025 describes Italy and Spain as "mature" markets, with Italy slowing. Globally, 3,282 new clubs opened in 2024, bringing the total to about 15,933 clubs and over 50,000 courts (+17%). Europe plus the UK have about 44,000 courts. Source: [The Padel Paper](https://thepadelpaper.com/playtomic-global-padel-report-2025/), [The Bandeja](https://thebandeja.com/playtomic-global-padel-report-2025/)
- FIP World Padel Report (2nd edition, mid-2025): over 35M players and about 77,000 courts worldwide. Spain has about 17,300 courts and France about 4,000. Source: [sgbonline](https://sgbonline.com/exec-study-finds-padel-boasting-robust-growth/). Europe is estimated at about 59% of players and about 70% of courts: [Racket Business](https://racketbusiness.com/p/padel-in-europe-a-three-lane-motorway-of-growth)
- Other countries (estimates): Spain about 5 to 6M players ([PalaHack](https://pala-hack.com/en/blog/global-padel-statistics/), [Tennis Creative](https://tenniscreative.com/padel-statistics/)). Sweden about 600k players and about 4,220 courts, with growth stalled ([Racket Business](https://racketbusiness.com/p/padel-in-europe-a-three-lane-motorway-of-growth)). France about 850k players, of whom 272k are licensed, with unclear methodology ([actu-padel](https://actu-padel.com/en/padel-statistics-how-many-people-play-this-sport-worldwide-in-2026/)).
- Federal padel tournaments in Italy, 2024: 1,367 tournaments, 62,541 participants. Team championships: 2,270 teams. Source: [Sport e Finanza](https://www.sportefinanza.it/2025/07/04/italia-report-fitp-numeri-tennis-padel-tesserati-appasionati-impatto-economico/)
- FITP-TPRA Padel "Road to Rome 2025" circuit: provincial qualifiers split into Expert and Entry levels by coefficient, then regional masters (top 8 per category per province) and a national master at the Foro Italico. Source: [TPRA Padel Road to Rome 2025 rules](https://dptpraprodfile.blob.core.windows.net/regolamenti/REGOLAMENTO%20TPRA%20PADEL%20ROAD%20TO%20ROME%202025.10.12.24.pdf)

### Inferences
- Most padel play in Italy happens outside federal tournaments: about 10k courts against about 1.4k federal tournaments a year. Club-run "americanas", leagues and booking-app tournaments (Playtomic and others) are a large adjacent market with no certification requirement. For a certified-scoresheet product, the federal and TPRA layer is the natural entry point.

### Gaps
- No count of TPRA padel tournaments or of non-federal padel tournaments in Italy was found.
- No count of Premier Padel or FIP tour events was gathered. These are professional and out of scope for amateur certification anyway.

## 3. Amateur tennis (and padel/pickleball) under TPRA

### Takeaway
TPRA (FITP's amateur ranking circuit for non-agonist members) is a large, mostly referee-free tournament layer: over 5,300 tournaments and over 80,000 entries in 2019. Recent national totals were not published in the sources found. The circuit now spans tennis, padel and pickleball, each with a "Road to Rome" or "Road to Torino" master format.

### Cited findings
- 2017: 896 clubs ran 3,602 FIT/TPRA amateur tournaments with 49,439 entries. Over 12,500 amateurs took part, up from 9,293 in 2016 and 5,514 in 2015. Source: [FITP TPRA course PDF](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/ISF/pdf_corsi_Dirigenti/corsi_2_grado/TPRA.pdf)
- 2019: over 80,000 entries and over 5,300 tournaments available. Source: [FITP "TPRA, un progetto nato per stupire"](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-nazionale/200203-Progetto-Tpra)
- One Road to Rome season (year not stated, probably about 2023): 25,000 total entries and 1,750 tournaments in Italian clubs. Source: [FITP Lazio, Master Nazionale TPRA Road to Rome](https://www.fitp.it/Siti-regionali/Lazio/News/News/Grande-successo-per-il-Master-Nazionale-TPRA-%E2%80%98Road-to-Rome%E2%80%99-La-festa-continua-al-Foro-Italico). This may cover the Road to Rome circuit only, not all TPRA activity.
- Veneto went from under 100 TPRA tournaments a year to 500 in 2024 (regional, interview 2025). Source: [TennisTalker magazine, Apr 2025](https://magazine.tennistalker.it/2025/04/17/tpra-scopriamo-il-tennis-amatoriale-che-sembra-professionismo/)
- Puglia, Jan to Apr 2021: 5,835 entries to TPRA tennis tournaments vs 1,327 in the same period of 2020. Source: [FITP Puglia](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-regionali/Il-TPRA-dilaga-in-Puglia-tennis-cresce-padel-esplode)
- Road to Rome 2025 (TPRA tennis): run by TPRA coordinators. After the third stage, the top two of each event in each province qualify for the regional master. Points feed a "power TPRA" used for FITP ranking. Source: [FITP Road to Rome 2025 rules](https://fitp.it/media/File_pdf/20250213_Regolamento_Circuito_Road_to_Rome_v2.pdf)
- TPRA amateur tournaments historically do not require a giudice arbitro, and draws are generated online automatically (2017 document, may be outdated). Source: [FITP TPRA PDF](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/ISF/pdf_corsi_Dirigenti/corsi_2_grado/TPRA.pdf)
- TPRA tennis general rules: [Regolamento generale TPRA Tennis 2024](https://tpra.fitp.it/File/REGOLAMENTO%20GENERALE%20TPRA%20TENNIS%202024%20-%20DEF_%2010_01_2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- In TPRA events the players or the club, not a referee, enter scores into the TPRA portal. This is the segment where a courtside self-entry tool with a player-confirmation "certified" scoresheet fits best: there is no official in the loop, and disputes and late entries are typical (unverified, no source found on dispute rates).
- Assuming TPRA grew in line with FITP membership (+266% 2020 to 2024), current TPRA volume is plausibly in the range of 5,000 to 10,000 tournaments a year. This is an assumption, not data.

### Gaps
- No 2024, 2025 or 2026 national TPRA totals were found (tournaments, entries, unique players). tpra.fitp.it and the FITP integrated report would be the sources.

## 4. Pickleball: Italy (FITP) and Europe

### Takeaway
Since 2024 to 2025 Italian pickleball sits under FITP, with a dedicated licence from 2025, a DUPR rating partnership, TPRA "Road to Rome" and "Road to Torino" circuits and team championships. Federal membership is small but doubling year on year (6,619 at end of March 2026, more than 15,000 projected for 2026). Already about 700 tournaments were played in Q1 2026. Europe's federation (EPF) has 38 member countries, but European player counts are poorly documented.

### Cited findings: Italy
- FITP pickleball members: 6,619 in Q1 2026, double the same period of 2025. Over 15,000 projected by end of 2026 (projection). Almost 700 tournaments played in the first three months of 2026. Players in all 20 regions, five of them with over 500 licences. Source: [FITP, "Pickleball, la strategia FITP accelera la crescita: numeri in forte aumento"](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Pickleball/Pickleball-la-strategia-FITP-accelera-la-crescita-numeri-in-forte-aumento). The search extract was fragmentary, so verify the exact wording.
- 2024 FITP first-year review: about 400 courts and about 10,000 entries or enthusiasts. Source: [Pickleballnet, 30 Oct 2024](https://pickleballnet.it/2024/10/30/fitp-bilancio-positivo/)
- 2024 status: experimental, non-exclusive discipline. A dedicated pickleball licence was introduced with the 2025 affiliation campaign, and from 2025 only FITP pickleball licence holders may play federal-sanctioned tournaments. Sources: [FITP affiliation campaign 2025](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-nazionale/campagna-affiliazione-tesseramento-2025), [PickleballSpot/pickleballtour.it](https://pickleballtour.it/news/la-stagione-2025-del-pickleball-in-italia-e-alle-porte-via-alla-campagna-di-affiliazione-e-tesseramento-fitp/)
- Circuits 2025: "Road to Torino" Open circuit with 4 Open tournaments (Foggia, Frascati, Marsala, Pescara, Jul to Oct) and a national master during the Nitto ATP Finals. Its 2nd edition (TPRA, ratings NC to 3.1) had one provincial stage per province per calendar date from 27 to 29 June 2025. Sources: [Road to Torino Open rules](https://www.fitp.it/media/Pickleball/20250619_Regolamento_e_programma_Circuito_Road_to_Torino___Open_Pickleball.pdf), [Road to Torino 2025 TPRA](https://www.fitp.it/media/File_pdf/20250910_Circuito_Road_to_Torino_2025_Pickleball_TPRA_v2.pdf)
- "Road to Rome 2025" pickleball: a non-agonistic circuit with provincial stages (28 Feb to 2 Mar, late March, mid April), regional masters on 25 to 27 April and a national master at the Foro Italico. Source: [FITP Lazio](https://www.fitp.it/Siti-regionali/Lazio/News/News/Pickleball-Circuito-Road-To-Rome-2025-il-calendario-e-le-sedi-di-gioco)
- Road to Roma TPRA (May 2026 article): over 7,400 participants in the Italian selections and about 300 qualified for the finals. The edition year is unclear. Source: [Calabria Pickleball, 18 May 2026](https://www.calabriapickleball.it/2026/05/18/road-to-roma-tpra-e-simone-salvatore-il-re-al-foro-italico/)
- First FITP pickleball team championship in 2025 (Open and Over 50). Source: [FITP team championship rules 2025](https://www.fitp.it/media/Pickleball/20251003_20251002_Regolamento_Campionati_a_squadre_Pickleball_2025.pdf)
- DUPR named official pickleball rating system for FITP. Source: [DUPR](https://www.dupr.com/es/post/dupr-named-official-pickleball-ratings-system-for-italys-fitp)
- Rome hosted the 2nd European Pickleball Team Championships (EPF) on 19 to 21 Sep 2025, with 30 nations. Source: [USR Lazio letter](https://www.ufficioscolasticoregionalelazio.it/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/PICKLEBALL-EUROPEI-2025_LETTERA-SCUOLE.pdf)
- Non-official estimates: a "1 million practitioners by 2026" target was announced in 2024 by a promoter. It is an aspiration, not data. Sources: [Pickleballnet, Jan 2024](https://pickleballnet.it/2024/01/16/pickleball-italia-praticanti/), [PickleballSpot](https://pickleballspot.eu/news/1-milione-di-giocatori-di-pickleball-in-italia-entro-il-2026/). Third-party guides give 200 to 350+ clubs and about 30,000 practitioners by 2026, with inconsistent figures: [bsness](https://www.bsness.com/analisi-di-mercato/analisi-mercato-pickleball/), [softwarebusinessplan.it](https://www.softwarebusinessplan.it/pickleball-guida-pratica/)

### Cited findings: Europe
- EPF: founded in Luxembourg on 31 May 2023 by 25 national associations, with 38 member countries as of Nov 2025. Sources: [EPF press release, 4 Nov 2025](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/EPF-Press-Release-4-11-2025.pdf), [Chronicle.lu](https://www.chronicle.lu/category/other-sports/45463-luxembourg-base-for-newly-formed-european-pickleball-federation)
- EPF became a Continental Member of the World Pickleball Federation (Aug 2026) and has also applied to the GPF. Source: [EPF press release, 4 Aug 2026](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/EPF-Press-Release-04-Aug-2026.pdf)
- Europe player counts vary widely. England is estimated at about 40k players ([The Dink](https://thedinkpickleball.com/pickleball-is-booming-in-the-uk-and-the-next-generation-is-leading-the-charge-2/)), and the UK at about 200k regular players in early 2026 (commercial blog estimate: [Racketrise](https://racketrise.co.uk/blog/pickleball-uk-statistics-2026)). The Pickleball Clinic report (Jan 2026) documents about 60k players for England and Spain combined (relayed via search). Global reach is claimed at 80 to 120M (industry estimate: [pickleball.com](https://pickleball.com/industry/global-pickleball-reach-hits-estimated-80-120-million-players)). Older baseline: about 12,000 players in Europe in 2020 ([SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/pickleball-is-spreading-to-europe/87673.article)).

### Inferences
- About 700 tournaments in one quarter makes pickleball in Italy, per tournament, already about half the size of federal padel (1,367 a year in 2024). Annualised, it could reach about 2,000 to 3,000 tournaments in 2026 (assumption: Q1 is not peak season). Pickleball tournaments are small (low entries), often round-robin, with many matches per player. That is ideal for courtside score entry and DUPR result submission.
- Italy is likely among the most structured pickleball markets in Europe because federal integration (FITP plus DUPR) is unusual. Elsewhere, national associations are typically independent of the tennis federation.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025 or 2026 counts were found for European pickleball players, clubs or tournaments by country. No list of AIP or other Italian non-FITP associations with figures was found.

## 6. How results are collected and certified today in Italian federal tournaments

### Takeaway
Agonistic FITP events must be directed by a Giudice Arbitro (GA), and non-agonistic ones by a Direttore di gara. The GA files a final report ("rapporto/referto") electronically or on FITP paper forms within 5 days. Team-championship results go into the federal SGAT system, by noon on the Monday after the match for padel, with fines for lateness. TPRA amateur events historically need no referee and use online draws. Score capture at court level is still largely manual (paper scoresheets or the GA keying in results), which is the gap for a courtside certified-entry product.

### Cited findings
- All authorised competitions must be directed by a GA (agonistic) or a Direttore di gara (non-agonistic). The GA report, filed electronically or on FITP paper forms, includes the event description, final results, seeds, notes on officiating and sanctions. It goes to the regional committee within 5 days of the end of the event. Source: [FITP Regolamento Tecnico Sportivo 2025](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Federazione/Federazione_Trasparente/20250801_RTS_con_modifiche_AAUU_di_luglio_2025_con_Appendice_XI___Padel_in_carrozzina.pdf)
- For events only "approved" by FITP, a paper referee report must be drafted. Source: [FITP RTS 2023/2025 edition](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Federazione/Federazione_Trasparente/20250305_RTS_con_modifiche_cf_del_14_febbraio_2025_con__Appendice_XI___Padel_in_carrozzina.docx.pdf)
- Padel team championships: the GA must enter the report online in SGAT by 12:00 on the Monday after the match and email a copy to the regional committee. Late or missing entries can be fined by the sports judge. Source: [Liguria Padel Serie D 2025 rules](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Siti_regionali/Liguria/File_pdf/Campionati_a_Squadre/PADEL/2025/20250430_Regolamento_Padel_serie_D_2025.pdf), [FITP padel team championship rules 2026](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Padel/File_Pdf/Campionati_a_squadre_2025/20260224_Padel__Regolamento_Campionati_a_squadre_2026_nuovo.pdf)
- Tennis inter-club (team) matches: the GA must report the result "immediately" via SGAT, or as the competent body directs if SGAT is unavailable. Failure is a disciplinary offence with a fine. Source: [Vademecum per gli Ufficiali di Gara 2025](https://www.udgfit.it/admin/public/modulistica/TUTTI_Vademecum_per_gli_Ufficiali_di_Gara_2025_20250218190841.pdf), [older Vademecum](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Carte_e_Atti/Carte/Vademecum-per-gli-Ufficiali-di-Gara.pdf)
- Tournament approval: clubs request approval via SGAT at least 30 days in advance so the event appears on the federal portal. Source: [FITP guide to organising a tournament](https://www.fitp.it/Circoli-scuole-e-insegnanti/Circoli/Guida-all-organizzazione-di-un-torneo/). SGAT manual for GAs: [udgfit.it](http://www.udgfit.it/admin/doc/manuale_SGAT_TI.pdf). GA forms portal: [UDG FITP modulistica](https://www.udgfit.it/modulistica.asp). GAAF manual 2025: [udgfit.it](https://www.udgfit.it/admin/public/modulistica/2025_Manuale___GAAF_20250218190959.pdf)
- Reported pain point: a federal portal update removed the online daily "ordini di gioco" (order of play) feature, which caused "non poche lamentele" among clubs. It was later restored (about 2019). Source: [FITP Veneto](https://www.fitp.it/Siti-regionali/Veneto/News/Comunicazioni/191208-Nuovo-servizio-ordini-di-gioco)
- Third-party tools already target this workflow. InCampo syncs FITP order-of-play times and produces results ready to be entered into the FITP portal at the end of the tournament. This implies manual re-keying into federal systems. Source: [InCampo](https://incampo.org/)
- TPRA: no GA required, and draws are generated online (2017). Source: [FITP TPRA PDF](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/ISF/pdf_corsi_Dirigenti/corsi_2_grado/TPRA.pdf)
- Pickleball: FITP results feed DUPR ratings. Source: [DUPR](https://www.dupr.com/es/post/dupr-named-official-pickleball-ratings-system-for-italys-fitp)

### Inferences
- The certification chain today is GA signature plus report, then SGAT entry, then the regional committee. Courtside, the match result is typically written on paper (draw sheet or scoresheet) and keyed in later by the GA or club, with double entry and a time lag (the 5-day and Monday-noon deadlines). A product that captures the score courtside with player/captain confirmation (digital signature) and exports to SGAT, TPRA and DUPR formats would address these steps. Adoption depends on FITP acceptance, because SGAT is the system of record.
- I found no public FITP API documentation, so integration with SGAT is likely a partnership or regulatory question.

### Gaps
- No published survey of GA or club pain points, dispute rates, or average time spent on result entry was found. Interviews with GAs and club secretaries would be needed.
- It is unclear whether FITP's "Match Point" or other newer official apps already offer courtside entry. No source was found.

## 7. Sports-tech and tournament software market size (Europe)

### Takeaway
Analyst estimates for "sports management software" vary by an order of magnitude ($1.6B to $12B globally in 2025) and do not isolate racket tournaments. A bottom-up niche estimate is more meaningful (section 8).

## 8. Bottom-up estimate: matches per year in Italian federal and TPRA circuits (estimates, with assumptions)

### Takeaway
Rough order of magnitude: about 0.8 to 1.2 million certifiable matches a year in Italy across FITP tennis, padel, TPRA and pickleball, played in about 10,000 to 15,000 tournaments a year at about 4,000 clubs. This is an estimate built from the 2024 official counts above plus stated assumptions.

### Cited inputs (official, 2024 unless noted)
- 6,352 tennis tournaments and 439,961 players involved; 1,367 padel tournaments and 62,541 participants; 13,103 tennis teams and 2,270 padel teams. Source: [Sport e Finanza / FITP Report 2024](https://www.sportefinanza.it/2025/07/04/italia-report-fitp-numeri-tennis-padel-tesserati-appasionati-impatto-economico/)
- TPRA: over 5,300 tournaments and over 80,000 entries (2019). Source: [FITP](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Attivita-nazionale/200203-Progetto-Tpra)
- Pickleball: about 700 tournaments in Q1 2026. Source: [FITP](https://www.fitp.it/Federazione/News/Pickleball/Pickleball-la-strategia-FITP-accelera-la-crescita-numeri-in-forte-aumento)
- About 4,000 to 4,100 clubs, 9,323 tennis courts, and about 5,300 FITP padel courts (2024 to 2025). Sources: [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/sport-economy/atp-finals-torino-2025-numeri/938742/), [AGI](https://www.agi.it/sport/news/2026-07-12/campi-da-padel-italia-37986662/)

### Inferences (assumptions labelled)
| Segment | Assumption | Matches per year (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| Tennis individual tournaments (FITP) | Knockout draw means matches ≈ entries − 1 per draw. Take the 439,961 entries; doubles entries are pairs, so they reduce matches slightly | about 380,000 to 440,000 |
| Tennis team championships | 13,103 teams × about 6 ties each ÷ 2 (two teams per tie), giving about 39,000 ties × about 4 rubbers (singles plus doubles) | about 120,000 to 200,000 |
| Padel individual tournaments (FITP) | 62,541 participants ≈ 31,000 pairs; group stage plus knockout gives about 1.0 to 1.5 matches per pair | about 30,000 to 50,000 |
| Padel team championships | 2,270 teams × 6 ÷ 2 × 3 matches per tie | about 20,000 |
| TPRA (tennis plus padel) | 80,000 entries in 2019 as a floor, growth ×1.5 to 2 since, about 1 match per entry (knockout) to 2 (round-robin) | about 120,000 to 300,000 |
| Pickleball (FITP plus TPRA) | About 2,000 to 3,000 tournaments a year (annualised from 700 in Q1 2026) × about 40 to 60 matches each (small round-robin events) | about 80,000 to 180,000 |
| **Total Italy** | | **about 0.75M to 1.2M matches a year** |

- Tournament count: 6,352 tennis + 1,367 padel + about 5,000 to 8,000 TPRA + about 2,000 to 3,000 pickleball gives about 15,000 to 19,000 events a year. Use about 10,000 to 15,000 as a conservative figure if TPRA has not grown since 2019 or if TPRA events are double-counted in FITP totals.
- Court-days: assume an average federal tennis tournament runs about 7 to 10 days on 3 to 4 courts, which gives about 6,352 × 30 ≈ 190,000 tennis tournament court-days a year. A per-court courtside device or tablet model would therefore need about 4,000 clubs × 2 to 4 courts of hardware or app seats.
- Indicative revenue pools (pure assumption, for scenario use only): at €0.30 to €1.00 per certified match, the Italian pool is about €0.25M to €1.2M a year. At €30 to €80 per tournament licence it is about €0.4M to €1.5M a year. At a €200 to €500 club subscription, 4,000 clubs give a theoretical maximum of about €0.8M to €2M a year. Federal-level contracts (FITP integration) would be the main lever.
- Europe scaling (rough): if Italy (1.25M FITP members) is about 10 to 15% of European federal racket-sport amateur competition volume (Germany, France, Spain, UK and the Netherlands each have large tennis federations; Spain dominates padel), the European certifiable match pool would be about 6 to 10M matches a year. This is a speculative multiplier; no source was found for European federation tournament counts.

### Gaps
- Precise 2025 tournament counts, TPRA volumes, draw formats and match counts were not published in the sources found, so all match figures above are modelled.
- Tournament and match counts were not found for other European federations (DTB, FFT, RFET, LTA, FEP).
