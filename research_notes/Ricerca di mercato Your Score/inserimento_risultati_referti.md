# Digital match-result entry and signed digital scoresheets: competitive landscape (racket sports and others), as of 22 Oct 2026

Scope note: research done 22 Oct 2026 with web search. Several primary pages (pickleball.com docs, volleyball.world, BTTV/ÖTV PDFs, rankedin.ladesk.com) could not be fetched directly (DNS errors from the research environment). For those, the findings rely on search-engine summaries of the page text, so they should be treated as "summary of primary source, not read in full". Vendor claims are marked as such.

## 1. Referee/umpire digital result entry in racket sports (pickleball, tennis, badminton, table tennis, padel)

### Takeaway
Referee point-by-point scoring apps are common and mature. At pro level they exist (Wimbledon/IBM tablets since the 1990s). At amateur level pickleball.com's free Referee App is the clearest example. They all record the score and push it to their own vendor's platform. None of the ones found produce a scoresheet that players sign or that is cryptographically certified.

### Cited Findings
- **pickleball.com Referee App (US, pickleball):** referees log in with their normal pickleball.com account. The tournament director issues a "tourney code" (from venue setup) and a "ref code" (set in Edit Tourney, acting as a password). The referee scores every point, the score appears live on the tournament site, and the referee "finalizes" the match so the result is posted. It needs an internet connection to publish live and has a Training Mode. — [pickleball.com docs: How to Use the Referee App](https://pickleball.com/docs/en/72000647302-how-to-use-the-referee-app.html); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1666312041); [pickleball.com blog](https://pickleball.com/blogs/take-advantage-of-the-referee-app-why-you-should-use-this-now-1)
- The Referee App only works with tournaments run on pickleball.com's own platform. It is a closed ecosystem, not platform-agnostic. — inferred from the access-code model in [pickleball.com docs](https://pickleball.com/docs/en/72000647302-how-to-use-the-referee-app.html)
- **Tennis, Grand Slam level:** Wimbledon chair umpires "use tablet computers to score the match". Each point shows up automatically on the scoreboards and wimbledon.com. The umpire's chair holds an IBM tablet. — [Wimbledon.com umpires A–Z](https://www.wimbledon.com/en_GB/atoz/umpires.html); [Wimbledon.com 2015 "A day in the life of an umpire's chair"](https://www.wimbledon.com/amp/en_GB/news/articles/2015-07-06/a_day_in_the_life_an_umpires_chair.html)
- Chair umpires at the 1994 US Open already kept score on IBM ThinkPad 730T pen tablets. — [Pen-Based Computing journal](https://www.penbasedcomputing.com/?p=5383); [Data Center Knowledge: tech behind the US Open](https://www.datacenterknowledge.com/business/the-technology-behind-the-u-s-open)
- **Tennis, amateur/academic:** a study from Central Java (Indonesia) describes an Android "digital score sheet" app for tennis referees that stores competition data in a web database. — [Journal UNNES, "Implementation of Digital Score Sheet... Tennis Referees"](https://journal.unnes.ac.id/journals/panjar/article/view/45794)
- **Badminton:** Zeetius is a tablet live-scoring system used by umpires and referees. The vendor says it has been used since 2018 at state, national and international events (vendor claim). — [Zeetius testimonials](https://zeetius.com/testimonials)
- **Table tennis:** the "Score Table Tennis" app (Double Yellow) lets users pick matches listed on tournamentsoftware.com. This is a third-party scoring app with a link to the tournamentsoftware ecosystem. — [Score Table Tennis listing](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.doubleyellow.tabletennis?l=en)
- **Badminton/multi-sport generic:** consumer scoring apps such as BadmintonPoints, Score Syncer and Badminton: Score Counter exist, with no federation integration found. — [BadmintonPoints](https://apps.apple.com/bb/app/badmintonpoints/id6747377276); [Score Syncer](https://apps.apple.com/mt/app/score-syncer/id6480135011); [IONSport iOnCourt](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1489506140)
- **Padel referee/scorer apps:** Padeuce uses a QR code printed and fixed to the court fence. Scorers scan it to open the app already tied to that court, the day's rules and teams load automatically, and spectators scan the same code for the live score. The listing says full brackets are "still being built". — [Padeuce, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6770645638)
- openPadel connects a phone to an on-court server by QR code to show live score, serve and tiebreak. It also has an offline mode. — [openPadel, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/openpadel/id6756242520)
- SPOSCOR Padel is a WordPress plugin for courtside phone scoring. Its tournament features need the paid Pro plan. — [WordPress.org SPOSCOR Padel](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=362138)
- Tournify (multi-sport, including padel) has built-in referee assignment. Pricing figures were not visible. — [Tournify padel](https://tournifyapp.com/en/sports/padel)
- The LTA (UK) padel tour requires the tournament organiser to engage a licensed referee. This is a regulatory hook: a "referee" role exists at amateur federal level. — [LTA Padel Tour Organiser Guide 2024](https://www.lta.org.uk/4a1b5d/siteassets/padel/lta-padel-tour-organiser-guide-2024.pdf)

### Inferences
- Referee apps are tied to the vendor (pickleball.com, IBM/Wimbledon, Zeetius). Their output is a live score feed, not a signed legal-style match record. The "Your Score" angle is the end-of-match signed record, not point-by-point scoring. Point scoring is crowded and often free.
- The QR-on-fence model (Padeuce) is the closest low-cost "one entry point per court" precedent in padel. It uses players' or scorers' own phones, not a dedicated tablet, and has no signatures.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the BWF and ITTF official umpire tablet systems (vendor, workflow, whether umpire or players sign). Searches for BWF/Visual Reality/TIS returned nothing relevant.
- Premier Padel / FIP referee scoring system: no source found in this session.
- Exact current hardware and vendor for Wimbledon/ATP/WTA chair-umpire scoring (IBM vs. others, e.g. ATP's own systems) is not confirmed by a current source.

## 2. Player self-reporting of results and opponent confirmation

### Takeaway
Player self-reporting with opponent confirmation is now standard in rating and booking ecosystems: pickleball.com, DUPR, Playtomic and probably Rankedin. Confirmation is a click on the player's own phone (accept/reject, or auto-validate after 24h). It is not a signature, not cryptographically certified and not portable between platforms.

### Cited Findings
- **pickleball.com player scoring:** players enter their own scores from a text (SMS) link. The opposing team confirms or disputes, and disputes go to the tournament desk. This can "auto-run" the tournament. — [pickleball.com docs: How to enable player scoring and auto-run](https://pickleball.com/docs/en/72000646262-how-to-enable-player-scoring-and-auto-run-your-tournament) (summary of page text; direct fetch failed)
- **DUPR:** a submitted match stays "pending" until opponents confirm by notification or email, and the rating updates once confirmed. In organised events, matches can be verified through connected platforms (e.g. Playbypoint) or manually in DUPR if the organiser allows it. — [Playbypoint help: Understanding DUPR](https://help.playbypoint.com/en/articles/11024893-understanding-dupr-dynamic-universal-pickleball-rating-for-players-and-managers); [DUPR: What is a DUPR rating](https://www.dupr.com/post/what-is-a-dupr-rating); [Pickleheads guide](https://www.pickleheads.com/blog/a-guide-to-pickleball-ratings-dupr-utpr) (secondary sources; DUPR's own help centre was not reached)
- **Playtomic (padel/tennis/pickleball, Europe leader):** players add the result in the app. It is "automatically validated within 24 hours". If an opponent accepts, it validates immediately. A rejected result is "suspended" and must be entered again. Playtomic "does not have the authority to compel players" and sends disputes to the match chat. Only valid, accepted competitive results change player levels. — [Playtomic player help: publish match results](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831755554449-How-to-publish-your-match-results-to-the-Playtomic-Community); [Playtomic: upload results from matches outside Playtomic](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19831532392081-How-to-upload-results-from-matches-played-outside-Playtomic); [Playtomic level system](https://playerhelp.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/43310980754193-How-the-Playtomic-level-system-works)
- Playtomic Manager added tournament tools (King of the Court, Americano, Mexicano) where round results are recorded and the leaderboard updates. No opponent-confirmation step was documented for tournaments. — [Playtomic Manager help: New Tournament Tools](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/44129657203985-New-Tournament-Tools-King-of-the-Court-Americano-and-Mexicano)
- **Rankedin:** has a help article "How players can enter results for Club League", which confirms player result entry exists. The approval details could not be read (fetch failed). — [Rankedin help 5.10.1](https://rankedin.ladesk.com/939096-5101-How-players-can-enter-results-for-Club-League)
- **Tournamentsoftware.com (Visual Reality; used by many badminton/tennis federations):** in a Munster Tennis (Ireland) league, the winning captain submits the results via "modify" → "Enter submatch results". The FAQ shows no opponent confirmation step. — [Munster Tennis results FAQ](https://www.munstertennis.ie/uploads/1/0/1/6/101627434/faq_results_sheet_v5_no_qr___add_player_section.pdf)
- **Tennis Australia League Manager:** depending on competition settings, results may need confirmation from the opposition and/or the administrator before becoming official. — [Tennis Australia support](https://support.tennis.com.au/?p=6722)
- **Tournify:** has a feature "Let teams or players enter scores". — [Tournify help](https://help.tournifyapp.com/en/articles/8909460-let-teams-or-players-enter-scores)
- **Padel Fast (GAVA Group AB, Sweden):** free download. Organising tournaments needs a subscription or premium purchase (no exact price found). Scoreboards can be shared by link, including on a venue TV. Supports 10+ formats (Americano, Mexicano, ladder...). Developer claims "59,000+ players and organizers" (vendor claim). — [PadelFast App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1607589873); [AppFollow listing](https://apps.apple.com/MX/app/id1607589873)
- **Other padel organiser apps with player/organiser score entry:** PadelScore (organisers invite players to log scores on their own devices) [listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/padelscoring/6779535280); Padel iD, RacketMix, Racket ID (record results, rankings) [RacketMix](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/racketmix/id6756780508), [Racket ID](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6480926395); Nà Padel (Italian) [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/n%C3%A0-padel/id1552765984); Padelino (free Americano/Mexicano) [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1244576/Padelino).
- **Italian amateur football (Play LND app):** results have three trust levels. A result entered by the home team's official is informative, the AIA referee report is "ufficioso" and the Giudice Sportivo approval is "ufficiale". — [PlayLND, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/playlnd/id1532851976)

### Inferences
- Opponent confirmation is a commodity feature, but each confirmation lives inside one platform (Playtomic levels, DUPR rating, pickleball.com brackets). A result confirmed in Playtomic does not become a portable, verifiable record for a federation.
- The Play LND three-level model (self-reported → referee → judge-approved) shows that federations already treat self-reported results as low-trust. This is the trust gap a signed, certified scoresheet could address.

### Gaps
- Not verified this session: Score7, World Padel Ranking, Score Padel, Racketlovers, Matchi, the FITP (Italian tennis/padel federation) tournament system, or Rankedin's exact confirmation rules and pricing. Searches for "Score7", "Score Padel" and "Racketlovers" returned no relevant primary pages.
- Rankedin, tournamentsoftware.com and Playtomic Manager pricing were not found.

## 3. Courtside tablet/kiosk per court

### Takeaway
Dedicated per-court hardware exists mainly as camera/analytics kiosks (PlaySight) or fence QR codes with players' phones (Padeuce, openPadel). No product was found that puts a dedicated tablet on every court for end-of-match result entry plus signatures.

### Cited Findings
- **PlaySight SmartCourt:** a courtside kiosk (PC + touchscreen) linked to 5–6 fixed cameras. It records strokes, trajectory, speed, spin and movement, and streams video and stats to the kiosk, tablets and phones. "PlayFair Tournaments" let players challenge line calls by checking replay on the kiosk. Per the ITF approval doc, players log in via the kiosk, and a tournament mode limits what is shown. Sources date from 2013–2018. — [Wikipedia: PlaySight Interactive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlaySight_Interactive); [ITF PAT approval report](https://m.itftennis.com/media/6220/pat-13-003-pat-approval-report-playsight-rev-0.pdf); [Sports Business Daily 2015](https://www.sportsbusinessdaily.com/Journal/Issues/2015/11/30/Research-and-Ratings/Company-Watch.aspx); [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/calgary/article/high-tech-training-tool-sets-new-standard-on-courts-at-alberta-tennis-centre/)
- **Padeuce:** QR code on the fence per court, with scorer and spectator modes (no dedicated tablet). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6770645638)
- **openPadel:** QR pairing with an on-court server. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/openpadel/id6756242520)
- **GameSheet (hockey):** rents "arena-ready iPads" for weekend events (price on quote). This is the closest precedent for a hardware-rental model for scoresheet tablets. — [GameSheet FAQ](https://www.gamesheetinc.com/resources/faq)

### Inferences
- PlaySight-style kiosks cost far more and do video analytics, which is not the same job as "Your Score". The cheap alternatives use the players' own phones. A dedicated, low-cost per-court tablet focused on result certification looks unoccupied in racket sports. Rental of tablets per event (as GameSheet does) is a proven model in another sport.

### Gaps
- Current PlaySight pricing and status in 2025–2026 (the company was acquired; ownership changes not verified this session).
- Button-based courtside scoreboards (e.g. padel/tennis scoreboards with remote buttons) were not researched in depth.

## 4. Electronic scoresheets with digital signatures (closest precedents, any sport)

### Takeaway
"Signing" a digital scoresheet is well established in German-speaking federations and in North American hockey/soccer. It is almost always done by **PIN entry** (nuScore, Handball SpielberichtOnline, DFBnet) or by **touch signature** (GameSheet). None of the sources describes qualified/cryptographic signatures, hashing, trusted timestamps or tamper-evidence. The integrity comes from the platform's access control ("after submission only an admin can edit").

### Cited Findings
- **nuScore (nu Datenautomaten; Germany/Austria, table tennis and tennis team leagues, linked to click-TT/nuLiga):** team captains are entered with the referee. After both captains check the report, it is "digitally signed" with the home and away match PINs and sent to the click-TT portal ("Absenden"). A report finished offline must be sent within the deadlines. In Austrian tennis (ÖTV 2025), each team needs its own PIN to "sign" results, the home captain enters on one device, and the away captain enters and signs their lineup with their own PIN. — [BTTV nuScore manual 2023-08](https://sr.bttv.de/fileadmin/bttv/media/000/mannschaft/click-tt/Handbuecher/nuScore_Anleitung_202308.pdf); [ÖTV/TTV nuScore Infos 2025](https://ttv.oetv.at/fileadmin/07_ttv/nuscore_Infos_2025.pdf) (summaries of PDF text; direct fetch failed); [Salzburg tennis description](https://www.salzburgtennis.at/fileadmin/05_stv/Mannschaftsmeisterschaft/Beschreibung_Homepage_2.pdf)
- **German handball, SpielberichtOnline (SBO, handball4all):** "instead of the previous signature on the match protocol, a PIN is used for identification". Each team needs a team PIN set in team administration. The team official makes entries, but the report is only released with the PIN. — [BWHV SBO E/F-Jugend Neckar-Alb](https://www.bwhv.org/fileadmin/bwhv/Spielbetrieb/Bezirk_NA/SpielberichtOnline_E-_und_F-Jugend_Bezirk_Neckar-Alb.pdf); [HVW OSPP training docs](https://www.hvw-online.org/fileadmin/hvw/Bezirke/bezirk_6/dokumente/SBO/OSPP_Schulungsunterlagen.pdf); [BWHV E-Jugend SBO](https://www.bwhv.org/fileadmin/bwhv/Spielbetrieb/Bezirk_NF/Kinderhandball/Ablauf_und_Info_fuer_E-Jugend_mit_SBO.pdf)
- **German football, DFBnet Spielbericht Online:** referees need a personal DFBnet login. Clubs release lineups, and the referee must complete and release the report (in Kreis Nienburg, within 1 hour of the final whistle). Some districts **still print part 1 for both team officials to sign by hand**, so the online release does not replace signatures everywhere. — [HFV referee handbook](https://www.hfv-online.de/fileadmin/user_upload/_imported/Handbuch_SR_Spielbericht_online.pdf); [SFV referee guide](https://www.sfv-online.de/fileadmin/content/PDFs/DFBnet/Spielbericht_Online/Spielbericht_Online_Anleitung_Schiedsrichter.pdf); [NFV Kreis Nienburg SR-Anweisungen 25/26](https://www.kreis-nienburg.nfv.de/fileadmin/Bezirke_Kreise/Kreis_Nienburg/Inhalt/Spielbetrieb/Schiedsrichter/2025-08-09_SR-Anweisungen_25-26.pdf); [NFV Hameln-Pyrmont short guide](https://www.kreis-hameln-pyrmont.nfv.de/fileadmin/Bezirke_Kreise/Kreis_Hameln-Pyrmont/News/Kurzanleitung-Spielb-Online.pdf); [DFBnet release note](https://portal.dfbnet.org/fileadmin/content/downloads/freigabe/sbo/150824_DFBnet-Spielbericht_Freigabemitteilung_R5_50-V11.pdf)
- **Italian football, LND "distinta elettronica":** a 2021 LND circular made electronic team sheets mandatory for official regional and provincial matches, prepared in the club's reserved area. "Firma elettronica" in LND refers to registration (tesseramento) and transfers, done by authorised club officials with a PIN and registered phone after a certification-body step. **No source found for digital signing of the match report itself**, and the AIA referee report remains a separate referee act. — [C.U. LND n.15 of 20 Sep 2021 (Lazio)](https://lazio.lnd.it/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/C.U.-LND-N.15-DEL-20-SETTEMBRE-2021.pdf); [LND Lazio guide to dematerialisation](https://lazio.lnd.it/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/guida-dematerializzazione.pdf); [LND Lazio informativa 2022](https://lazio.lnd.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/041_02_09_2022_Informativa-alle-Societa.pdf)
- **Italian volleyball (FIPAV):** "Referto Elettronico by Newbit" is a free iPad app that fills in the electronic match report for games linked to PortaleFIPAV (no mention of captain signatures in the listing). Since 2020 FIPAV has worked with InfoCert (a qualified trust service provider) to give club presidents qualified digital signatures/Business Keys for documents, but not for match sheets. — [Newbit App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/referto-elettronico-by-newbit/id1553475908); [Forbes Italia, FIPAV and InfoCert, 20 Nov 2020](https://forbes.it/2020/11/20/fipav-e-infocert-la-collaborazione-digitale-passa-anche-dallo-sport/)
- **International volleyball (FIVB/CEV):** on the paper scoresheet, captains sign to confirm the lineup and at the end. The FIVB e-Scoresheet (eScorMan manual) uploads the official scoresheet data to a central database at match end. CEV made Data Project's DataVolley mandatory for all CEV matches (2015 doc, laptop-based). FIVB tested an electronic scoresheet in beach volleyball at Lucerne (2020). **How captains sign electronically was not confirmed.** — [Volleyball Ireland eScorMan manual](https://www.volleyballireland.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/eScorMan.pdf); [CEV Data Project guidelines](https://inside.cev.eu/media/eulhmot0/dataproject-software-guidelines.pdf); [volleyball.world Lucerne e-scoresheet test](https://www.volleyball.world/en/beachvolleyball/worldtour/2020/229/news/video-electronic-score-sheet-testing-at-lucerne?id=52046); [USA Volleyball International Scoring Manual](https://usavolleyball.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/International-Scoring-Manual.pdf)
- **Basketball (FIBA Digital Scoresheet, DSS):** FIBA describes a Windows application developed by FIBA that replaces the paper scoresheet and produces a PDF. The official page says nothing about tablets or signatures. The Table Officials Manual has a DSS user manual section that was not read. — [FIBA Digital Scoresheet](https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/data-and-video-solutions/digital-scoresheet); [FIBA TO Manual 2019 v3.0](https://ksv.rs/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/FIBA_TO_MANUAL_2019-Feb_v3.0.pdf)
- **Italian basketball (FIP):** in FipOnline, referees send the result (recorded as "ufficioso") and upload reports later. — [FIP Basilicata, designations system](https://basilicata.fip.it/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Nuovo-sistema-integrato-di-gestione-delle-designazioni.pdf)
- **GameSheet (North America; hockey, lacrosse, soccer):** coaches and officials sign digitally on the device (iPad for hockey, iPhone for the soccer app). Coaches and referees get a post-game email with a link to the digital scoresheet. In hockey, the scorekeeper and timekeeper sign first, then all officials sign. **After submission only a league administrator can change the sheet.** It works offline, prints Hockey USA/Hockey Canada-approved sheets and rents iPads. Pricing is quoted per case ("less than a new hockey stick per team"). Some governing bodies pay for it (Hockey New Brunswick 2021-22). — [GameSheet digital scoring app](https://gamesheetinc.com/solutions/digital-scoring-app); [GameSheet Soccer, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6479320089); [Hockey Calgary RAMP GameSheet instructions](https://www.hockeycalgary.ca/assets/file/Operations/League_Management/RAMP/RAMP_GameSheet_Instructions_-_Scorekeepers.pdf); [GameSheet FAQ](https://www.gamesheetinc.com/resources/faq); [Hockey New Brunswick partnership](https://hnb.ca/en/list-of-latest-news/hockey-new-brunswick-and-gamesheet-inc-announce-partnership-for-the-2021-2022-season); [Spordle scorekeeper guide](https://spordle.atlassian.net/wiki/x/QwD0sQ)
- **GES Digital Scoresheet (basketball referee app):** match results and referee accounting, with no signatures mentioned. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ges-digital-scoresheet/id6740844186)

### Inferences
- The strongest precedent for "Your Score" is **nuScore** (each team signs by PIN on one shared device at the venue, works offline and syncs to the federation portal) together with **GameSheet** (touch signatures from all parties on one shared tablet, locked after submission). Both are tied to one league platform (click-TT/nuLiga; GameSheet's own stats). Neither claims a qualified timestamp, a hash chain or a tamper-evident export that a third-party platform could verify.
- In Italy, LND and FIPAV already use e-signatures (InfoCert, PIN+OTP) for administrative acts, but not for match reports. Italian federations are therefore used to qualified e-signatures (eIDAS), which could make a certified-scoresheet pitch credible.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed that FIVB VIS, CEV/DataProject or the FIBA DSS capture captain/coach signatures electronically, or how.
- EHF (European handball) international e-scoresheet: not researched.
- Pricing for nuScore/nuLiga (usually licensed to federations) was not found.

## 5. Pricing models observed

### Takeaway
Player-facing result entry is mostly free, bundled into booking or rating platforms. Organiser tools are freemium or subscription. Federation scoresheet systems are licensed to the federation or league and free for clubs. Few public price points exist.

### Cited Findings
- pickleball.com Referee App: free download. The listing shows no separate referee fee. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1666312041)
- Padel Fast: free download, with a subscription or premium purchase needed to organise tournaments (price not public in sources). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1607589873)
- SPOSCOR Padel: free plugin, with a paid Pro plan for tournaments. — [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=362138)
- Padelino: free. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1244576/Padelino)
- Referto Elettronico by Newbit (FIPAV): free iPad app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/it/app/referto-elettronico-by-newbit/id1553475908)
- GameSheet: per-team pricing on quote ("less than a hockey stick per team"), depending on level, teams, games and players. iPad rental on quote. Sometimes paid by the governing body. — [GameSheet FAQ](https://www.gamesheetinc.com/resources/faq); [HNB](https://hnb.ca/en/list-of-latest-news/hockey-new-brunswick-and-gamesheet-inc-announce-partnership-for-the-2021-2022-season)
- Tournify: has a pricing page, but no figures were captured. — [Tournify padel](https://tournifyapp.com/en/sports/padel)

### Inferences
- A per-court tablet product would need either hardware rental or bring-your-own-tablet plus a SaaS fee. GameSheet shows both models working, with federations sometimes paying.

### Gaps
- Public prices for Rankedin, Playtomic Manager, tournamentsoftware.com, Matchi, Score7, DUPR organiser plans and PlaySight were not found in this session.

## 6. What nobody seems to do (gap analysis)

### Takeaway
No product was found, in racket sports or elsewhere, that combines all four: (a) a dedicated courtside tablet per court, (b) signatures from **all** players (not just captains or officials), (c) cryptographic certification (trusted timestamp, hash, tamper-evident export, e.g. eIDAS-qualified) and (d) platform-agnostic delivery to any tournament platform. Each element exists somewhere on its own.

### Cited Findings
- (a) per-court entry point: Padeuce/openPadel fence QR (players' phones) [Padeuce](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6770645638); PlaySight kiosk (analytics, not result certification) [ITF report](https://m.itftennis.com/media/6220/pat-13-003-pat-approval-report-playsight-rev-0.pdf)
- (b) multi-party sign-off on one device: nuScore PINs per team [BTTV](https://sr.bttv.de/fileadmin/bttv/media/000/mannschaft/click-tt/Handbuecher/nuScore_Anleitung_202308.pdf); GameSheet touch signatures [Hockey Calgary](https://www.hockeycalgary.ca/assets/file/Operations/League_Management/RAMP/RAMP_GameSheet_Instructions_-_Scorekeepers.pdf); handball SBO PINs [BWHV](https://www.bwhv.org/fileadmin/bwhv/Spielbetrieb/Bezirk_NA/SpielberichtOnline_E-_und_F-Jugend_Bezirk_Neckar-Alb.pdf)

The only cross-platform links seen are DUPR accepting verified matches from connected platforms such as Playbypoint, and Score Table Tennis reading tournamentsoftware.com draws. — [Playbypoint/DUPR](https://help.playbypoint.com/en/articles/11024893-understanding-dupr-dynamic-universal-pickleball-rating-for-players-and-managers); [Score Table Tennis](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.doubleyellow.tabletennis?l=en)

### Inferences
- The unoccupied spot is a **neutral "result notary" layer**: a certified, all-player-signed match record that any platform (Playtomic, Rankedin, tournamentsoftware, pickleball.com, DUPR, federation portals) could ingest and verify. Its value grows where there is no referee (club and amateur federal circuits) and disputes or sandbagging affect ratings and rankings.
- Risks for "Your Score": (1) incumbents already offer "good enough" confirmation for free, and players may not want to sign on a shared tablet after every match. (2) Platform-agnostic only works if platforms build integrations or accept imports; DUPR's connected-platform model shows this is possible but has to be negotiated. (3) German federations show that PIN-based "signing" is enough for regulators, so qualified certification has to justify its cost, for example for prize-money events, rating integrity or disputes.

### Gaps
- Could not rule out niche players such as Premier Padel/FIP internal tools, FITP (Italy) systems, BWF/ITTF umpire tablet systems, EHF e-scoresheet, Score Padel, Score7, World Padel Ranking or Racketlovers. Searches did not surface primary material for these, so the "nobody does it" conclusion is limited to what was found.
