## A1. Pickleball: who trains and certifies referees and tournament staff (EPF, USA Pickleball, Pickleball England, FITP, others), and at what cost

### Takeaway
Pickleball refereeing in Europe is already standardised: the EPF Referee Academy uses the USA Pickleball (USAP) curriculum and credentials, which are mutually recognised. Cost to a candidate is about EUR 265 (EUR 10 membership + EUR 75 Part I + EUR 180 Part II). In Italy FITP runs pickleball competitions with FITP Giudici Arbitri but I found no dedicated FITP pickleball referee course. I found no formal pickleball tournament-director or "desk staff" certification anywhere: this is the clearest gap.

### Cited Findings
**EPF (European Pickleball Federation)**
- EPF "Referee Academy" is run "in alignment with" USA Pickleball. The EPF follows USAP officiating policies and best practices, aiming at one global referee standard, and the two bodies recognise each other's referee levels and testing requirements — [EPF Referee Academy](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/referee-academic-certifications/); [EPF Refereeing FAQ](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=1378)
- Levels: Level 1, Level 2, Certified (the highest credential, for all levels of play including pro matches). A Level 1 EPF referee is recognised as Level 1 in the US and Canada — [EPF Referee Level 1 course](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2337); [EPF Referee Structure](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=1208)
- 2026 editions: Level 1 courses January–March 2026 (England, Belgium, Germany); Level 2 course March 2026 (Germany) — [EPF L1](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2337); [EPF L2](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2852); [EPF L2 (alt.)](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2876)
- Format: three online classes with Gail Scavongelli / the USAP team (e.g. Mondays 7pm CET, 5/12/19 January), with recordings for anyone who misses a class. Then online tests, self-organised practice at clubs or tournaments, and an on-court assessment — [EPF L1 course listing](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2372); [EPF L1](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2042)
- Fees: EUR 10 EPF membership + EUR 75 for Part I. Candidates who pass Part I pay EUR 180 for Part II, due at the latest one week before the on-court assessment (total ≈ EUR 265) — [EPF L1 course listing](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2348)
- Gail Scavongelli runs USA Pickleball's referee training programme and leads the EPF courses — [EPF L1](https://europeanpickleballfederation.org/?p=2372)

**USA Pickleball**
- Path: Verified Trainee → Level 1 → Level 2 → Certified Referee. Level 1 requires USAP membership, classroom training from a Registered Referee Trainer, an eye exam (at least 20/30), a minimum age of 16, and an assessment by a Certified Referee or trainer. Level 2 adds a Tiered Referee Rating (TRR) and an Advanced Training Session (ATS) — [Pickleball.com help: How do I become a certified referee](https://pickleball.com/docs/en/article/how-do-i-become-a-certified-pickleball-referee); [USAP Referee Q&A](https://usapickleball.org/member-news/referee-training-and-certification-qa); [USAP referee pages](https://usapickleball.org/referee/entry/16381)
- USAP runs FREE Zoom courses, for example three 2-hour sessions on 3 consecutive days in May 2024. Some online tests require USAP membership — [USAP online referee training PDF](https://usapickleball.org/docs/referee/Online-referee-training-may-6-8-2024.pdf)
- The certification application fee covers "the credentials package, program administration and evaluator travel expenses". No dollar amount was visible in the results — [USAP Ref Cert Application Fee](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/ref-cert-app-fee/)
- Background on referee pay and training at pro level — [The Dink: training and compensation behind pickleball's referees](https://www.thedinkpickleball.com/training-compensation-and-other-details-behind-pickleballs-dedicated-referees/)

**Pickleball England**
- Uses the EPF referee pathway, and points people interested in later cohorts to the EPF — [Pickleball England Referee Pathway](https://www.pickleballengland.org/referee-pathway)

**Italy – FITP**
- FITP pickleball championships (e.g. 2026 individual championships, national phase 11–14 June 2026) are run by a FITP **Giudice Arbitro**, who draws up the groups — [FITP Regolamento campionati individuali pickleball 2026](https://www.fitp.it/media/Pickleball/20260519_Regolamento_campionati_individuali_pickleball_2026_v3_.pdf)
- For the national phase the Giudice Arbitro is paid by FITP and gets a dedicated desk at the host club — [FITP richiesta assegnazione fase nazionale 2026](https://www.fitp.it/media/Pickleball/20260211_richiesta_assegnazione_fase_nazionale_campionati_individuali_pickleball_2026_2.pdf)
- FITP training for pickleball covers **instructors** (Istruttore di 1° livello pickleball): 60 places per course, part online and part in person. The 2023 fee was EUR 100 — [FITP bando IS1 Pickleball 2023](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/News/Allegati_Pdf/Varie/20230922_2023_IS1_PICKLEBALL_STRAO.pdf); [FITP IS1 Pickleball Sardegna 2024](https://www.fitp.it:443/media/File_pdf/20240313_IS1_PICKLEBALL_REV_ES_Sardegna.pdf)
- FITP's January 2026 document lists a pickleball section and ISF training courses for 2026–2027, but no pickleball-specific referee course was visible — [FITP gennaio 2026](https://www.fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Federazione/News/Circoli_e_Scuole/20260119_FITP_gennaio_2026_NEW.pdf)
- Comparison: Spain's RFET runs a "Juez Árbitro Nacional de Pickleball" course, which requires the "Juez de Pista Nacional de Pickleball" course first — [RFET normativa docencia](https://www.rfet.es/descargar/file-docencia-cursos-titulaciones/50_Normativa/normativa.pdf)

### Inferences
- In Europe, on-court refereeing is a "closed" credential chain (USAP/EPF). A private Academy should not issue a competing "pickleball referee" credential. It would collide with EPF/USAP and FITP and confuse the market.
- The open space is in roles **nobody certifies**: tournament desk/director operations (draws, scheduling, live scoring), digital scorekeeping on tablets, and technicians for camera line-calling. These match Your Score's product.
- At ~EUR 265 for an EPF referee and EUR 100 for a FITP instructor, a complementary micro-certification (e.g. EUR 49–149 online) sits within the price range this audience already pays. This is an inference, not market-tested.
- In Italy FITP is the gatekeeper: pickleball is under FITP, and FITP Giudici Arbitri run the official events. Any FITP-sanctioned use of Your Score's tools would run through them.

- No EPF/USAP course dates for 2027 were found.
- No evidence was found of any formal pickleball tournament-director certification (USAP, EPF, Pickleball England, FITP). This absence may be real or a search limitation; verify directly.
- Other Italian pickleball bodies (e.g. associations outside FITP, EPS sports-promotion bodies): not researched in depth.

## A4. Would federations accept a co-branded complementary certification? Precedents of tech vendors certifying operators

### Takeaway
Yes, there is a clear precedent, but always under the federation's umbrella. FIFA/IFAB certify VAR officials and Replay Operators through the IAAP, while vendors (EVS, Broadcast Solutions) sell operator training and simulators. Hawk-Eye co-designed review-official training camps with the USTA, ATP, WTA and ITF. The ITF certifies the *systems* (ELC classification), not the operators. Co-branding works when the federation owns the credential and the vendor provides the tool-specific training.

### Cited Findings
- Hawk-Eye and the USTA ran the first "review official" training camp for Electronic Line Calling events, with ATP, WTA and ITF experts. It was designed for international recognition across systems — [Hawk-Eye news: training camps for review officials](https://www.hawkeyeinnovations.com/news/hawk-eye-%26-professional-tennis-organisations-begin-series-of-training-camps-for-review-officials)
- The ITF classifies ELC systems (Hawk-Eye rated Gold) through a 3-phase evaluation: lab, tournament "shadow" test, live test. Poor performance can lead to suspension. This approves the system, not the operator — [ITF Classified ELC systems](https://www.itftennis.com/en/about-us/tennis-tech/classified-elc-systems/); [ITF line calling PDF](https://itftennis.com/media/7203/line-calling.pdf)
- An ITF approval report describes the Hawk-Eye operator's role: sending challenged calls to the display after the review official authorises it — [ITF PAT report Hawk-Eye](https://m.itftennis.com/media/6216/pat-13-002-pat-approval-report-hawk-eye-rev-6.pdf)
- FIFA/IFAB's IAAP sets standards and oversees training for people who use VAR systems — [FIFA VAR technology standards](https://fto.tickets.fifa.com/innovation/standards/video-assistant-referee/video-assistant-referee-technology)
- EVS sells a 2-day "Xeebra VAR replay operator" course — [EVS training](https://evs.com/services/training/customer/operators/xeebra-var-replay-operator-training). Broadcast Solutions and EVS sell a "VAR Kick-off Pack" with a simulator to help federations certify VARs and Replay Operators — [Broadcast Solutions](https://broadcast-solutions.de/en/news/article/broadcast-solutions-and-evs-launch-the-var-kick-off-pack-to-help-federations-certify-their-officials-to-video-assistant-refereeing/)

## B1. eIDAS / Italian CAD: is a handwritten signature on a tablet (firma grafometrica) valid, and what gives it evidential value?

### Takeaway
Under eIDAS any electronic signature, a tablet scribble included, cannot be refused as evidence just because it is electronic (art. 25). In Italy, though, a simple electronic signature is "freely assessed" by the judge. To get the full value of a private written document (art. 2702 c.c.), the graphometric signature must be set up as a **Firma Elettronica Avanzata (FEA)**: signer identification, sole control, document integrity after signing, plus the AgID/DPCM 22/02/2013 technical rules (signed document, hash, timestamp, audit trail, and obligations on the FEA provider). [LAWYER]

### Cited Findings
- eIDAS art. 25(1): an electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect or admissibility as evidence solely because it is electronic or not qualified. This does **not** make it equal to a handwritten signature — [ValidSign legal framework](https://validsign.eu/resources/legislation); [Axiocap legal regime](https://www.axiocap.com/en/legal-summary/legal-regime-electronic-signatures)
- eIDAS art. 26: an advanced electronic signature (AES) must (a) be uniquely linked to the signer, (b) be able to identify the signer, (c) be created with data the signer controls with a high level of confidence, and (d) be linked to the signed data so that any later change is detectable — [Adobe Acrobat Sign eIDAS whitepaper](https://www.adobe.com/content/dam/cc/en/trust-center/compliance/other/document-cloud/Acrobat_Sign_eIDAS_wp.pdf); [Truescreen AES guide](https://truescreen.io/articles/advanced-electronic-signature-eidas-guide/). Primary text: [Reg. (EU) 910/2014 on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj); eIDAS 2 amendment: [Reg. (EU) 2024/1183](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj)
- CAD art. 20(1-bis): an electronic document meets the written-form requirement and has art. 2702 c.c. effect when signed with a digital, qualified or **advanced** signature. "In all other cases" its fitness and evidential value are **freely assessable in court** according to quality, security, integrity and immutability — [Agenda Digitale](https://www.agendadigitale.eu/?p=35295); [EC News](https://www.ecnews.it/legale/procedura-civile/processo-civile-telematico/codice-dellamministrazione-digitale-processo-luno-non-esclude-laltro/)
- Commentators hold that eIDAS did not change the Italian CAD/technical rules on FEA, so the national FEA rules (DPCM 22 Feb 2013) are still the operational basis — [ICT Security Magazine](https://www.ictsecuritymagazine.com/articoli/biometria-firme-elettroniche/)
- FEA requirements under the technical rules: signer identification, the signer's sole control over the signing system (including biometric data), and verification that the document has not been changed after signing — [InfoCert istruzioni soggetto erogatore](https://www.infocert.it/pdf/firma/istruzioni_soggetto_erogatore.pdf)
- Example of an FEA provider's operating manual (obligations of the "soggetto erogatore": user terms, informativa, insurance, retention) — [Dip. Trasformazione Digitale – manuale operativo erogatore FEA](https://assets.innovazione.gov.it/1687184449-manuale_operativo_erogatore_fea_v1-3-1-62.pdf)
- Real-world graphometric FEA manuals (banks): encrypted biometric vector embedded in the PDF, keys held by a third party — [BNL Manuale Tecnico Operativo](https://bnl.it/rsc/SupportingFiles/Firma-Grafometrica-Manuale-Tecnico-Operativo.pdf); [Deutsche Bank Italia](https://hongkong.db.com/italia/documents/Firma_Grafometrica_-_Manuale_Operativo.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a match scoresheet, Your Score can choose between two levels:
  1. **Simple electronic signature (FES) + strong audit trail.** Signature image, identity of the logged-in player/referee, timestamp (ideally a qualified timestamp), SHA-256 hash of the final scoresheet, device/GPS/IP log, and a locked PDF/A with a seal. Legally admissible; value freely assessed by the judge. Probably enough for sports purposes. [LAWYER]
  2. **FEA graphometric.** Biometric vector capture (pressure, speed) encrypted in the document, plus FEA provider obligations. Gives art. 2702 c.c. value but adds heavy GDPR biometric compliance (B2). Probably overkill for a scoresheet.
- Practical alternative to biometric FEA: an OTP/SMS or app-based FEA or click-to-sign with identity verified at registration, or a **qualified electronic seal** applied by Your Score/the federation to the final referto (it guarantees origin and integrity without player biometrics). [LAWYER]
- Under the eIDAS rules, a qualified electronic timestamp carries a presumption of accuracy of date and time. That is background knowledge, not verified in this session; see the EUR-Lex text, art. 41.

### Gaps
- The eIDAS article texts were not fetched directly (EUR-Lex not checked). Secondary sources disagree on the paragraph numbering of the AES definition, art. 3(11) vs 3(10).
- No case law found on electronic sports scoresheets as evidence.

## B2. GDPR: is a tablet signature biometric data? Garante 2014 provvedimento; minimal obligations for names + signatures; minors

### Takeaway
A plain signature *image* is ordinary personal data. A **graphometric** signature, which captures pressure, speed and acceleration, is biometric data (art. 4(14) and art. 9 GDPR), and the Italian Garante set specific rules for it in its general provvedimento of 12 Nov 2014. If Your Score captures only a signature image, standard GDPR obligations apply. If it captures dynamic data, art. 9 safeguards, a DPIA, encryption, an alternative method and the Garante 2014 measures apply. [LAWYER/DPO]

### Cited Findings
- Garante general provvedimento on biometrics and graphometric signatures, 12 Nov 2014 (GU n. 280 of 2 Dec 2014), sets out obligations for biometric processing and for signing electronic documents with a graphometric signature — [ICT Security Magazine](https://www.ictsecuritymagazine.com/articoli/biometria-firme-elettroniche/); [Univr summary](https://docs.univr.it/documenti/Documento/allegati/allegati093951.pdf). Primary: [Garante docweb 3556992](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/3556992) (could not be fetched in this session)
- Earlier Garante decision (2013, banking): graphometric signature allowed provided it qualifies as FEA under the CAD and DPCM 22/02/2013 — [Filodiritto](https://www.filodiritto.com/garante-privacy-si-alla-firma-grafometrica-i-servizi-bancari-purche-sia-una-firma-elettronica-avanzata)
- The Garante treats samples and templates derived from the tablet's pressure, speed and acceleration as biometric data. Banks still cite the 2014 provvedimento in their 2025 notices — [ICT Security Magazine](https://www.ictsecuritymagazine.com/articoli/biometria-firme-elettroniche/); [Cassa di Risparmio di Volterra informativa FEA 09/2025](https://www.crvolterra.it/wp-content/uploads/Informativa-FirmaEA_WEB_V2-092025.pdf)
- Operators state that using a graphometric tablet means processing biometric data, which the rules treat as "sensitive" — [Synlab informativa tavoletta grafometrica](https://www.synlab.it/images/file/Intormativa%20e%20consenso%20Tavoletta%20Grafometrica.pdf)
- Per EDPB criteria (as reported), biometric processing counts as special-category data for both 1:N identification and 1:1 authentication. The EDPB also expects controllers to offer a non-biometric alternative — [Chambers Data Protection 2025 – Spain](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2025/spain/trends-and-developments); [GDPR Summary – biometric data](https://www.gdprsummary.com/?p=189)

### Inferences
- Obligations even without biometrics (names + signature image + results): informativa (art. 13); legal basis, likely contract/legitimate interest/compliance with federation rules rather than consent; defined retention; processor agreements (art. 28) with clubs/federations; security; records of processing. Public results/rankings need their own assessment.
- With graphometric capture: art. 9(2) exception, in practice explicit consent plus a non-biometric alternative; DPIA (art. 35); encryption of the biometric vector with keys held separately; strict retention. The Garante 2014 measures apply too. Keeping the 2014 provvedimento in mind post-GDPR follows market practice, but its formal status after D.Lgs. 101/2018 should be confirmed. [LAWYER/DPO]
- Minors (background knowledge, verify): art. 8 GDPR, with Italy setting age 14 in art. 2-quinquies of the Codice Privacy, covers consent to information-society services. For under-14 players a parent/guardian should consent and sign. Avoid biometric capture for minors altogether. [LAWYER]
- Recommendation: design for "signature image + audit trail + seal", with **no** dynamic biometric capture by default. This keeps Your Score out of art. 9.

### Gaps
- The full Garante 2014 text was not retrieved. Its current status after the GDPR is not confirmed.
- No EDPB/Garante guidance found that explicitly says a static signature image is not biometric.
- Italian minors' age rules were not verified via a source in this session.

## B3. Sports-justice angle: do federation rules require paper scoresheets or referee signatures? Federations already accepting electronic referti

### Takeaway
FITP rules make the Giudice Arbitro (or the Direttore di gara in non-agonistic events) responsible for the referto. The text found allows it to be drawn up "con procedura informatica o sui moduli cartacei predisposti dalla F.I.T.", so electronic referti are already contemplated, but through FITP's own procedure or forms. In Italy, FIP (basketball) already uses an official tablet Referto Digitale (InGame by NBN23), including coaches' signatures on the tablet. Electronic referti are therefore accepted in Italian sport when the federation adopts the tool.

### Cited Findings
- FITP technical rules: the Giudice arbitro, or the Direttore di gara in non-agonistic events, draws up the referto "con procedura informatica o sui moduli cartacei predisposti dalla F.I.T.", with mandatory attachments. Agonistic events need a GA; non-agonistic events need a Direttore di gara. The version is unverified (from a clause aggregator) — [LawInsider clause "Referto arbitrale"](https://lawinsider.com/it/clause/referto-arbitrale); [LawInsider RTS](https://lawinsider.com/it/contracts/lK3cY00tnZP); official RTS: [FITP RTS Sept 2023](https://fitp.it/media/FIT/Federtennis/Federazione/Federazione_Trasparente/20230929_20230928_RTS_con_modifiche_cf_di_settembre_2023_clean.pdf)
- FIP basketball "InGame – Referto Digitale" (NBN23) is the official app for tablet/smartphone. The manual recommends a tablet of at least 10.1", iOS 16 or Android 13+, and 4 GB RAM. The app downloads the match, collects **coaches' signatures**, syncs online, sends the final data, and can resume a match on another device. The manual does not describe a legal-grade (FEA) signature — [FIP Marche manual Jul 2026](https://marche.fip.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MANUALE-INGAME-Referto-Digitale.pdf); [FIP Lazio manual Aug 2026](https://lazio.fip.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/MANUALE-INGAME-Referto-Digitale.pdf)
- FIPAV (volleyball) gave club presidents digital-signature tools (InfoCert Business Key) for federation paperwork, not match referti — [Forbes Italia: FIPAV e InfoCert](https://forbes.it/2020/11/20/fipav-e-infocert-la-collaborazione-digitale-passa-anche-dallo-sport/)

### Inferences
- Sports justice in Italy rests on federation rules, not civil-law signature requirements. A referto's value before the Giudice Sportivo comes from the official's role (and the federation-adopted procedure), not from an eIDAS level. A FITP/EPF-adopted Your Score referto with the official's authenticated sign-off and an immutable log would likely be enough. Player signatures add acknowledgement value but are not the core of it. [LAWYER, sports law]
- Route to adoption: get FITP to recognise Your Score as an approved "procedura informatica" (the NBN23–FIP basketball precedent) rather than chasing FEA-level legal value.
